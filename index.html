Compound Kid

Here's a fact most people never feel until it's too late to use it: money that compounds doesn't grow in a straight line — it grows in a curve. Flat for a long time. Then, suddenly, steep.

Compound Kid is a free, one-tap arcade game built around that single curve. You run, you jump, you collect coins — and every coin you collect doesn't just add to your score, it compounds. The longer you survive, the faster your value climbs. Most players don't believe the number on their screen until they've seen it happen to themselves.

It takes ten seconds to understand and about that long to lose your first run. That's the point. You'll be back.
